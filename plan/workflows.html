Service Workflows

The middle back office is the administrative path from incoming demand through approved booking, customer updates, completed-job records and routine billing follow-through. It does not include performing field work, designing systems, making technical diagnoses, supervising safety or acting as the contractor of record. Client-approved instructions are the operating authority; each exception has a named client recipient and fallback.

Task assignment rule: deterministic software enforces required fields and explicit approved rules; artificial intelligence proposes summaries, category labels and drafts; trained humans approve customer-facing judgments; clients retain licensed, safety, money-moving and exception authority. No customer address, access code or payment information goes into a general consumer chatbot. Redact/minimize data and review provider contract terms before production use.

Concrete use cases: (1) overflow caller requests maintenance; operator checks released slot, confirms location and books in the client's system; outcome is a valid appointment rather than a message. (2) an approved estimate is unanswered; operator makes permitted contact, records objection and routes a technical question to the estimator; outcome is a verified next step, not a manufactured sale. (3) completed job lacks notes; coordinator returns it to technician; invoice waits for client approval. Edge cases: caller reports gas odor: use client-approved urgent safety script and emergency/on-call handoff; never troubleshoot. Client schedule has no capacity: record waitlist or offer approved later option; never book a fictional slot. An invoice is disputed: stop collection script and escalate.
