Execution

The minimum viable service is sellable with trained people, the client's field-service account, phone routing, approved scripts and a secure queue. It does not depend on a custom portal or autonomous voice system. Critical path: named operating lead → actual capacity/cost allocation → customer records and signed scope → permissions and configuration → certified simulations → live pilot work → closed performance/economic observation → renewal → repeatable capacity. Market interviews, legal scoping, platform checks and training preparation can proceed in parallel when they do not require the same customer records or app control.

All effort ranges below are active team work estimates, not elapsed schedules or deadlines. They overlap in skills and may reuse existing materials; do not add them mechanically to infer a completion date.

Do not add a second trade/platform/time zone simultaneously. Change only one of trade, platform, or time zone at a time. Preserve the scorecard and measure whether the change reduces margin or increases exceptions. A stage requirement can be met earlier or later than a planning horizon; only closed evidence advances the business.
