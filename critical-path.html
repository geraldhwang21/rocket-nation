The critical path

No scaled launch before paid demand, safe delivery, measured contribution, and renewal. This graph shows decision dependencies. It does not assign completion dates or imply any requirement has been met.

Accountable lead & committed capacity

One operating lead. Confirm the people, productive cost, coverage, and staged learning budget.

Evidence: named owners, actual costs, approved allocation. Prerequisite for the paid offer.

↓

Customer records

Inspect permissioned call, estimate, schedule, and invoice records. Establish a baseline and field capacity.

Depends on: a qualified prospect, approved initial data terms, and client permission.

Paid commitments

Win three bounded paid pilots near the proposed price. Document scope and the economic buyer.

Depends on: customer diagnosis, costed scope, and approved initial contract, data and contact rules.

Safe operating setup

Apply approved contract and data terms to each client. Confirm access, consent, client authority, escalation contacts, and tested fallback.

Depends on: approved initial boundaries and agreed pilot scope. Client-specific configuration and readiness checks follow. Live access waits for approval.

↓ All three converge before live delivery

Certified people & live pilot

Rehearse normal and failure cases. Certify operators. Start controlled traffic and record workload, errors, outcomes, and every recurring cost.

Evidence: client configuration approval, tested routing, backup coverage, complete measurement records.

↓

Quality passes

Meet the defined quality standard. No unresolved critical safety, privacy, or financial error.

If it fails: stop the affected workflow and correct the cause.

Value & cost pass

Show attributable client contribution and fully costed service contribution. Do not count a booking as collected profit.

If it fails: narrow scope, change price, or stop.

Renewal passes

Obtain at least two standard-price renewals and repeat the work with another trained operator.

If it fails: diagnose value, trust, price, or delivery before adding clients.

↓ All three must pass

Controlled expansion

Release the next capital tranche. Add clients in measured cohorts. Add automation only where the pilot proves its value.

We decide separately whether to enter new trades, expand nationwide, or replace the field-service platform.

Blue marks the investment decisions. Dark nodes show required evidence. All nodes are proposed work, not completed operating milestones.

Work that can run in parallel

Platform & security

Verify authorized platform access, minimum permissions, call-routing fallback, data terms and integration rights. Do not connect production records before approval.

People & quality

Build the task taxonomy, scripts, escalation rules, training cases, quality rubric, and backup plan. Use simulated cases until live access is approved.

Sales & evidence

Prepare the paid offer, diagnostic, consented baseline, pilot scorecard, and buyer review. Record rejected offers as evidence too.

Rules for approval

What proves willingness to pay?

Three paid commitments from qualified buyers near the intended price. Free trials, friendly interest, and nonbinding letters do not establish a repeatable price.

What blocks live work?

Unclear authority, missing data terms, unsafe access, untested escalation, no trained backup, or no way to reconcile the client's records.

What justifies scaling?

Quality, attributable customer value, measured workload and cost, at least two standard-price renewals, a funded cash bridge, and delivery that another certified specialist can repeat.

What is deliberately deferred?

A new field-service platform, autonomous dispatch or technical diagnosis, a technician app, a permit engine, and a shared client-data training pool.
