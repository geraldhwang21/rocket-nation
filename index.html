    How the figures are calculated
    • New clients pay the setup fee and are onboarded in the month they sign. The monthly service fee starts the next month. Clients lost in a month still pay for that month.
    • Staff are hired in whole people. Philippines specialists = (hours per client × billing clients + training hours × new clients) ÷ productive hours per specialist, rounded up, never below the minimum. Supervisors = specialists ÷ span, rounded up. India engineers and United States account managers follow the same rule with their own hours.
    • Per-client figures share people fully, so they cost fractions of a person. Delivery cost = specialists + supervision + software and AI. Gross profit = fee − delivery cost. Contribution = gross profit − account and analysis time − integration upkeep.
    • Sales cost per new client = commission and campaign cost + (salespeople cost + marketing budget) ÷ new clients per month. Months to earn it back = (sales cost + any onboarding cost above the setup fee) ÷ contribution.
    • Client gross profit vs sales cost = gross profit × expected billed months within the projection ÷ sales cost per new client. No discount rate is applied anywhere.
    • Break-even = smallest steady client count whose monthly fees, plus setup fees from the clients signed to replace those lost, cover all costs, with the sales team kept.
    • Most cash invested = the lowest point of cumulative cash, including the launch cost. The plan is run forward up to 240 months to find it, so it can fall after the months shown. All cash back by = the month cumulative cash turns positive and stays positive, found in the same 240-month run.
    • Cash recovered by the final month = cumulative cash in that month minus its lowest point, shown against the most cash invested. Annual operating profit at the final month's pace = that month's operating profit × 12.
    • Excluded: taxes, financing, late payment, bad debt, overage fees, price changes and wage increases. Fractional clients are averages, not actual sign-ups.
