## Limits

- This is content research only. No layouts, imagery, brand styling, animation or website design are recommended for reuse.
- HTML was parsed without browser execution. Scripts and asset references are archived and counted; script logic, stylesheets, image pixels, video/audio, canvas rendering, embeds and interaction states were not exhaustively inspected. Image alt attributes are recorded; the core inventory exposed logo alt text and empty alts.
- Private client portal, external recruitment/booking services, external social platforms/review systems, form submission and authenticated content were excluded. No form was submitted and no message was sent.
- Unlinked public pages absent from the sitemap and reachable links may exist; historical versions were not exhaustively searched. A sitemap is a discovery aid, not proof that no other content exists.
- The Cloudflare email-protection route was unreachable as business content (HTTP 404).
- Claims of certification, reviews, scale, growth, security, results, zero management burden and platform expertise remain first-party assertions. No production execution, contract or certificate audit was performed.
- Every element of the entire rendered website has NOT been demonstrated as read twice. The precise supported claim is two retrieval/inventory passes plus the documented semantic reading scope.
